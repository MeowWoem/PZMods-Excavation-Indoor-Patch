description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Tired of digging a perfectly good basement with Excavation, only to have it register as "outside" forever?
description=This patch fixes that.
description=
description=[h2]What does it do?[/h2][list]
description=[*] Excavated basement tiles now properly register as an [b]indoor room[/b] (correct ambient temperature, no more "outside" readings underground).
description=[*] Rooms are created automatically right after digging (square or stairs).
description=[*] Fixes a game-crashing [b]NullPointerException[/b] that occurs when leaving a dug-out basement and moving far enough away for the game to reprocess the area.
description=[*] Fixes a side effect of that crash fix: zombies could spawn directly inside a sealed, freshly-registered room. They can no longer spawn there.
description=[*] Rooms survive save/reload — they're rebuilt automatically as the chunk loads, not when you happen to walk over the tile again.
description=[*] Safe to add to an existing save: already-dug basements get patched the moment you walk through them.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Why does this exist?[/h2][list]
description=[*] Excavation carves out underground space but never creates a real [i]RoomDef[/i] for it, so the game treats it as outdoors.
description=[*] Forcing a room onto an excavated tile turns out to be trickier than it looks: the game's low-level room-editing API doesn't register what it creates the way the game's own cleanup logic expects, causing a crash whenever the surrounding terrain keeps changing (i.e. whenever you keep digging).
description=[*] Working around that crash safely, without breaking save persistence or letting zombies spawn where they shouldn't, ended up needing several independent fixes chained together.
description=[/list]
