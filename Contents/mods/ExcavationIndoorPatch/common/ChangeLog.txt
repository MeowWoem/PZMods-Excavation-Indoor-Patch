[ v1.1.0 - 09.20.2026 ]
Changed:
- Reworked interior room creation: a dug basement is now a single room instead of one room per square.
- A newly dug square next to an existing dug basement is added to its room, and digging a square that connects several dug basements merges them into one room.
- Dug basements are never merged with vanilla basements or buildings.
- A "duplicate BuildingDef.metaID" error may now appear in the logs when rooms are merged. It is expected and can be safely ignored (a message is logged beforehand to say so).

Fixed:
- The single room rework fixes the bed "Sleep" option not appearing unless you were standing in the same room as the bed.
- Light switches built in a dug basement now light up the whole room instead of only the square they were placed on.
